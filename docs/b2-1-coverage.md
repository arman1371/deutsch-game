# B2.1 – syllabus research, coverage and overlap with A1.1–B1.2

B2.1 was built from the first half of the B2 syllabus of the course books listed below, checked against the Goethe-Zertifikat B2
model exam. The rule from A2.2, B1.1 and B1.2 still applies: **teach what is new, extend what an earlier unit started, and only
review – inside new activities, never as a stand-alone unit – what is already taught.**
`src/content/b2-1/content.test.ts` enforces the vocabulary side: no word card in B2.1 repeats a word card of A1.1–B1.2 (or
another B2.1 unit).

The German content has **not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.**

Unit ids: `b2-1-beziehungen`, `-besitz`, `-wohlbefinden`, `-kommunikation`, `-teamgeist`, `-lebenswege`, `-gesellschaft`,
`-wohnen`, `-medien`, `-wissenschaft`, `-unterwegs`, `-klima` (units 1–12, 173 games, 376 word and phrase cards).

Limits of the research:

- **Not retrievable:** the contents of Menschen B2.1, Netzwerk neu B2, em neu, Mittelpunkt neu B2 (the page was down) and
  Profile Deutsch could not be fetched, so none of them is used as evidence.
- **No clean half-year split:** Aspekte neu B2 (10 chapters), Mit uns B2 (12 lessons) and the DVV list (20 lessons) do not publish
  a B2.1 / B2.2 split. The first half of each was used as a guide, and where a topic only appears in the second half of one book,
  it counts only if another source teaches it in a first half.
- **B1 books end at B1:** Schritte international Neu stops at B1, so it is not used for B2.1.
- **Contents pages, not the books:** all sources are tables of contents, publisher descriptions or exam documents. The vocabulary
  and the texts were written for this app; no copyrighted course-book text was used.

## Grammar hints

B2.1 follows the hint system of PR #8: every grammar gap-fill and challenge question carries a `why` sentence for its wrong
options, and 16 new mini lessons were written for the B2.1 grammar tags (`nouns-with-prepositions`, `middle-field`,
`konjunktiv-1`, `causal-expressions`, `assumptions`, `es-dass`, `state-passive`, `temporal-connectors`, `noun-verb-phrases`,
`relative-wer`, `conditional-b2`, `text-cohesion`, `passive-alternatives`, `auch-wenn`, `adversative`, `nominalisation`).

## Engines

B2.1 uses flashcards, quiz, memory, listen, spell, sentence, gap fill, sort it, conjugation (unit 2: Konjunktiv I; unit 4:
Futur II; unit 5: Zustandspassiv), blitz and dialogue. The clock and market engines are not used.
