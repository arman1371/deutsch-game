# C1.2 – syllabus research, coverage and overlap with A1.1–B1.1

C1.2 is the second half of the C1 syllabus. It was built from the second halves of C1 course books and from
university C1.2 syllabi, checked against the CEFR C1 descriptors and the structure of the Goethe-Zertifikat C1.
The rule from A2.2 and B1.1 still applies: **teach what is new, extend what an earlier unit started, and only
review – inside new activities, never as a stand-alone unit – what is already taught.**
`src/content/c1-2/content.test.ts` enforces the vocabulary side: no word card in C1.2 repeats a word card of any
earlier level that exists on `main` (the test picks up every level whose id sorts before `c1-2` automatically), or
another C1.2 unit.

The German content has **not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.**

Unit ids: `c1-2-finanzen`, `-gesundheit`, `-recht`, `-psychologie`, `-stadt`, `-literatur`, `-kino`, `-geschaeft`,
`-forschung`, `-team`, `-nachhaltigkeit`, `-vortrag` (units 1–12, 156 games).

## How the units were chosen

A topic became a unit when at least two independent sources teach it in the second half of C1 (or, for the C1
Band 1 / first-half material, when it is clearly not covered by a C1.1 unit and fits C1.2). The grammar of a unit
is what those sources attach to the topic, minus anything A1.1–B1.1 already teach.

## Overlap with earlier levels

- **Gesundheit** (u2) is not B1.1's _Gesund bleiben_: it adds medical and food-safety vocabulary and the past
  infinitive; **Recht** (u3) continues B1.1's passive with modal verbs towards passive substitutes.
- **Stadt und Dorf** (u5) and **Nachhaltigkeit** (u11) extend the housing and environment topics of A2.1 / B1.1 with
  planning, farming and data vocabulary; no word card is repeated (checked by the test).
- Vocabulary that clashed with an earlier word card (for example _Schulden_, _Währung_, _Zeuge_, _Erfindung_) was
  replaced by a more specific word; the words still appear in sentences.
- Sibling levels (B1.2, B2.x, C1.1) that are merged later are compared automatically by the same test; if a word
  card clashes, replace it in the later level.
