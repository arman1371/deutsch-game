# B1.2 – syllabus research, coverage and overlap with A1.1–B1.1

B1.2 was built from the second half of the B1 syllabus of the course books listed below, checked against the Goethe-Zertifikat
B1 task types. The rule from A2.2 and B1.1 still applies: **teach what is new, extend what an earlier unit started, and only
review – inside new activities, never as a stand-alone unit – what is already taught.**
`src/content/b1-2/content.test.ts` enforces the vocabulary side: no word card in B1.2 repeats a word card of A1.1–B1.1 (or
another B1.2 unit).

The German content has **not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.**

Unit ids: `b1-2-kollegen`, `-digital`, `-konsum`, `-weiterbildung`, `-regeln`, `-engagement`, `-politik`, `-geschichte`,
`-stadt-land`, `-heimat`, `-kunst`, `-diskussion` (units 1–12, 149 games).

## Pronunciation

Two games carry the **Pronunciation** skill tag: sentence melody (rising for yes / no questions, falling for statements and
W-questions, from the Netzwerk neu B1 phonetics clip „Satzmelodie“, K8) in unit 2, and stress in compound nouns in unit 10.

## Grammar hints

B1.2 follows the hint system of PR #8: every grammar gap-fill and challenge question carries a `why` sentence for its wrong
options, and nine new mini lessons were written for the B1.2 grammar tags (`je-desto`, `als-ob`, `participles-as-adjectives`,
`modal-particles`, `indem-ohne-dass`, `passive-past`, `relative-wo-was`, `nicht-position`, `consecutive-clauses`). The mini
lessons for the B1.1 grammar tags, which were missing on `main` (the lesson test was failing), were added at the same time.

## Engines

B1.2 uses flashcards, quiz, memory, listen, spell, sentence, gap fill, sort it, conjugation (unit 8: generated Passiv
Präteritum and Perfekt tables), blitz and dialogue. The clock and market engines are not used.
