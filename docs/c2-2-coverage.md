# C2.2 – syllabus research, coverage and overlap with A1.1–B2.2

C2.2 is the second half of the C2 level. It was built from every C2 document that could be found online and
read, checked against the CEFR C2 descriptors. The rule from the earlier levels still applies: **teach what is
new, extend what an earlier unit started, and only review – inside new activities, never as a stand-alone
unit – what is already taught.** `src/content/c2-2/content.test.ts` enforces the vocabulary side: no word card
in C2.2 repeats a word card of A1.1 to B2.2 (or another C2.2 unit).

The German content has **not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.**

Unit ids: `c2-2-kunst`, `-literatur`, `-kulturerbe`, `-recht`, `-staat`, `-engagement`, `-klima`, `-nuancen`,
`-stellung` (units 1–9).

## What C2 material exists (and what does not)

C2 is not covered like A1–B1: there is no Profile Deutsch for C2, the Goethe C2 handbook deliberately lists no
topics, and few C2 coursebooks publish a table of contents online. The topic list below is therefore built from
**seven sources of different kinds**, and a topic only became a unit when **at least two of them** support it.

**Limits of the research.** Profile Deutsch stops at B2. _Mittelpunkt C2_ and _Ziel C2_ tables of contents could
not be retrieved, so _Erkundungen C2_ (via a university syllabus) is the only C2 coursebook contents list; _Kontext
C1_ is used as a cross-check for the grammar. The two university syllabi (F, B) are course descriptions, not
coursebooks. The School-Scout page is a preview that lists chapter titles only. All of this is why the unit list
is anchored on topics and grammar that appear in several different kinds of source.

## How the work was split with C2.1

C2 was cut in two by the order of the sources (earlier chapters and topics → C2.1, later ones → C2.2) and
agreed with the C2.1 thread so the levels do not overlap:

| C2.1 (first half)                                                                                                                         | C2.2 (this level)                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Topics: language / communication / style, working life, technology and AI, health and medicine, money and economy                         | Topics: art and aesthetics, literature, heritage and rituals, law, state and politics, civil society, globalisation and climate, idioms, arguing                           |
| Grammar: Konjunktiv I (reported speech), Konjunktiv II / Futur II, adverbial clauses and connectors, Nomen-Verb-Verbindungen, Wortbildung | Grammar: participial attributes, subjective modal verbs, passive in all tenses and its alternatives, Gerundiv, formal genitive prepositions, prefix verbs, modal particles |

C2.1 keeps its tag ids under the `c21-` prefix; C2.2 uses `c22-`.

## Grammar overlap with A1.1–B1.1

| Syllabus item               | Earlier levels                                                          | Treatment in C2.2                                                                              |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Participles as adjectives   | adjective endings in A2.2; B1.2 (PR #13) teaches the basics             | extended attributes and relative-clause transformation (u1); Partizip II of sein-verbs         |
| Modal verbs                 | objective use in A1.1, A2.2, B1.1 (passive with modal verbs)            | **new:** subjective meaning, perfect infinitive, modality verbs (u2)                           |
| Passive                     | Präsens A2.2 u7, with modal verbs B1.1 u3; Präteritum / Perfekt in B1.2 | Plusquamperfekt, Futur I, Zustandspassiv, worden vs. geworden (u3)                             |
| Infinitive with zu          | B1.1 u5                                                                 | **new:** zu + Partizip I (Gerundiv) and sein + zu (u4)                                         |
| Genitive prepositions       | wegen, trotz, während, innerhalb, außerhalb, anlässlich in B1.1         | **new:** angesichts, infolge, aufgrund, seitens, hinsichtlich, mittels, zwecks, zugunsten (u5) |
| Separable verbs             | A1.1–A2.1                                                               | **new:** verbs with a separable and an inseparable reading (u6)                                |
| Alternatives to the passive | lassen A2.2 (as causative only)                                         | **new:** sich lassen, sein + zu, -bar / -lich adjectives, man, bekommen-Passiv (u7)            |
| Modal particles             | B1.2 (PR #13) teaches doch, mal, eben                                   | extended to nuance: halt, wohl, schon, immerhin, ohnehin, bloß, etwa, eigentlich (u8)          |
| Speech acts of discussion   | B1.2 (PR #13) has a discussion unit                                     | **new level of register:** concede, qualify, refute, conclude (u9)                             |

## Game types

Only the existing engines are used. Each unit has 15–16 games covering flashcards, quiz, memory, listen, spell,
sentence, gap fill, sort it, conjugation (units 2, 3, 6), blitz and dialogue. Grammar units carry a mini lesson
per grammar tag (`src/content/lessons.ts`) and a `why` line for every wrong option.
