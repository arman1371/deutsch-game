# B2.2 – syllabus research, coverage and overlap with A1.1–B2.1

B2.2 was built from the second half of the B2 syllabus of the course books and university B2.2 syllabi listed below, checked
against the Goethe-Zertifikat B2 model test and the CEFR B2 descriptor. The rule from A2.2 to B1.2 still applies: **teach what
is new, extend what an earlier unit started, and only review – inside new activities, never as a stand-alone unit – what is
already taught.** `src/content/b2-2/content.test.ts` enforces the vocabulary side: no word card in B2.2 repeats a word card of
A1.1–B2.1 (or another B2.2 unit).

The German content has **not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.**

Unit ids: `b2-2-beziehungen`, `-ernaehrung`, `-studium`, `-service`, `-gesundheit`, `-sprache`, `-wirtschaft`, `-kunst`,
`-geschichte`, `-stadt`, `-druck`, `-netz` (units 1–12, 173 games).

Limits of the research:

- **Menschen B2.2** (Hueber) and **Netzwerk neu B2** (Klett) tables of contents could not be retrieved, so they are **not** used as
  evidence.
- **Ziel B2/2** (Hueber): only fragments of lessons 9–11 were readable; not used.
- **Aspekte neu B2 Teil 2**: chapter titles only, no grammar.
- The **Vielfalt B2.2** Stoffverteilungsplan was machine-read and partly jumbled; a few topic / grammar pairings are inferred from
  its layout, and lesson titles are in the publisher's English summary.
- **Kontext B2.2** grammar comes from a Spanish university course programme that lists it per module, not from the printed book.

## How the units were chosen

topic, minus anything A1.1–B1.2 already teach. B2.1 was built in parallel from the same books, so B2.2 shares some grammar with it (see below).

### Overlap with B2.1

B2.1 (PR #16) was researched in parallel from the same course books. B2.2 therefore shares **no word cards** with it (the
vocabulary-integrity test now includes B2.1), but five grammar tags are taught in both levels: `konjunktiv-1`, `nominalisation`,
`noun-verb-phrases`, `passive-alternatives` and `relative-wer`. B2.2 reuses B2.1's tag and mini lesson for them and practises the
point again in new topics. Related points that B2.1 also covers under other tags (Zustandspassiv, concessions, contrasts,
assumptions) are likewise a second, deeper pass.

## Grammar hints

B2.2 follows the hint system of PR #8: every grammar gap-fill and challenge question carries a `why` sentence for its wrong
options, and eighteen new mini lessons were written for the B2.2 grammar tags (listed above). A lesson is shown automatically when
an activity has exactly one specific grammar point; mixed activities set `lesson` on the item or show no hint.
`COVERS` in `src/content/lessons.ts` was extended so a B2.2 lesson also covers broader earlier tags (e.g. `konjunktiv-1` covers
`konjunktiv-2`).

## Engines used

flashcards, quiz, memory, listen, spell, sentence, gapfill, sortit, conjugation (Konjunktiv I tables), blitz and dialogue. No new
engine was added; clock and market have no B2.2 content.
