# A2.2 – syllabus coverage and overlap with A1.2 / A2.1

A2.2 was built from a syllabus merged from several course books (Netzwerk, Schritte plus, Menschen, Panorama,
Momente and university A2.2 syllabi). The rule for the pack: **teach what is new, extend what an earlier unit
started, and only review – inside new activities, never as a stand-alone unit – what is already taught.**
`src/content/a2-2/content.test.ts` enforces the vocabulary side: no word card in A2.2 repeats a word card of
A1.1, A1.2 or A2.1 (or another A2.2 unit).

Unit ids: `a2-2-lernen`, `-sport`, `-nachbarn`, `-kunst`, `-wuensche`, `-feste`, `-post`, `-werbung`, `-reisen`,
`-bank`, `-leben`, `-arbeit` (units 1–12, 190 games).

## Vocabulary topics

Learning, exams and presentations (u1) · sport and fans (u2) · housing conflicts, moving and pets (u3) · music
and painting (u4) · time (u5) · festivals and clichés (u6) · post, telephone and mobile phones (u7) · goods,
advertising and shopping habits (u8) · traffic and travel (u9) · banking, money and insurance (u10) · life
stages (u11) · office life, friendship, accidents, hospital, choosing a job or training (u12).

Weather, school, job search, media and technology, health and weekend events were already part of A1.2 / A2.1
and are only used in new contexts. **Politics** appears as a handful of words in u6 and is otherwise left for B1.

## Pronunciation

Pronunciation is trained with the existing engines and a new **Pronunciation** skill tag (four games):

- sentence stress – quiz „Which word is stressed?“ (u1)
- intonation of questions – quiz „Does the voice rise or fall?“ (u2)
- final b, d, g – sort-it with audio (u6)
- r versus l – listen & pick with minimal pairs (u9)

The app cannot record or grade speech, so these are listening and recognition tasks.

## Engines

A2.2 uses flashcards, quiz, listen, spell, sentence, gap fill, sort it, conjugation, blitz and dialogue. The
memory, clock and market engines are not used in A2.2; they stay available for later levels.
