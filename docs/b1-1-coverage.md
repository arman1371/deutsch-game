# B1.1 – syllabus research, coverage and overlap with A1.1–A2.2

B1.1 was built from the first half of the B1 syllabus of four widely used course books, checked against the
CEFR B1 descriptors and the Goethe-Zertifikat B1. The rule from A2.2 still applies: **teach what is new,
extend what an earlier unit started, and only review – inside new activities, never as a stand-alone unit –
what is already taught.** `src/content/b1-1/content.test.ts` enforces the vocabulary side: no word card in
B1.1 repeats a word card of A1.1, A1.2, A2.1 or A2.2 (or another B1.1 unit).

The German content has **not yet been reviewed by a native speaker**.

Unit ids: `b1-1-glueck`, `-unterhaltung`, `-gesundheit`, `-sprachen`, `-beruf`, `-service`, `-wohnen`,
`-freundschaft`, `-zukunft`, `-feiern`, `-umwelt`, `-pannen` (units 1–12, 173 games).

## How the units were chosen

A topic became a unit when at least two of the B1.1 books teach it; the grammar of a unit is what those books
attach to the topic, minus anything A1.1–A2.2 already teach.

## Pronunciation

Three games with the **Pronunciation** skill tag, taken from the Netzwerk neu B1 phonetics clips and the usual
B1 phonetics topics: n / ng / nk with audio (u1), consonant clusters such as Herbst, Obst, Angst (u3) and word
stress in foreign words such as Universität, Telefon, Information (u4).

## Engines

B1.1 uses flashcards, quiz, memory, listen, spell, sentence, gap fill, sort it, conjugation (with new generated
tables for the Plusquamperfekt, Futur I, Konjunktiv II of the past and the passive with modal verbs in
`b1-1/helpers.ts`), blitz and dialogue. The clock and market engines are not used.
