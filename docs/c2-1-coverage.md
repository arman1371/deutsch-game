# C2.1 – syllabus research, coverage and overlap with A1.1–C1.1

C2.1 is the first half of the C2 level (C2.2 is built in a sibling thread). The rule from A2.2 to B1.2 still applies: **teach
what is new, extend what an earlier unit started, and only review – inside new activities, never as a stand-alone unit – what
is already taught.** `src/content/c2-1/content.test.ts` enforces the vocabulary side: no word card in C2.1 repeats a word card
of A1.1–C1.1 (or another C2.1 unit). B2.1, B2.2 and C1.1 were merged while C2.1 was in review; the 95 C2.1 word cards that repeated
their vocabulary were replaced with new words.

The German content has **not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.**

Unit ids: `c2-1-sprache`, `-arbeit`, `-wirtschaft`, `-digital`, `-ki`, `-medizin`, `-psyche`, `-medien`, `-debatte`
(units 1–9, 122 games).

## What the research could and could not find

C2 is thinly documented compared with B1: the Goethe-Zertifikat C2 description **deliberately lists no topics**, and no C2
course book publishes a full table of contents online (Hueber's _Cosmos C2_ promises "12 thematic lessons" without titles;
_Mittelpunkt_ stops at C1; "Texte, Themen und Strukturen" is a German school book for the Oberstufe, not a DaF C2 course).
The unit list therefore rests on the sources below, which are the best that could be retrieved, and on a rule: **a topic became
a unit only when at least two independent sources point to it** (two courses of the same university count as one source).
Where the evidence comes from the C1 course books (Ziel C1) it is marked, because they are the bridge to C2, not C2 itself.

## How C2 was split into C2.1 and C2.2

No source divides C2 into halves (the sources teach C2 as one level), so the split is a decision, agreed with the C2.2 thread:
it follows the order in which the C2 course material introduces things (_Erkundungen C2_ chapters 1–3 before 4–6) and keeps
the units balanced.

| Half                         | Topics                                                                                                                                                                                        | Grammar and register                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **C2.1** (this level)        | language, communication and style · working life · money and economy · digitalisation · AI and research · medicine · mind and wellbeing · media and public opinion · debate and presentations | Konjunktiv I (reported speech) and Futur II · Konjunktiv II in hindsight and unreal conditions · adverbial clauses and connectors (adversative, causal, consecutive, concessive) · nominal style (clause ↔ prepositional phrase) · noun-verb combinations · word formation · text cohesion |
| **C2.2** (sibling, not here) | art and aesthetics · literature · culture, history and rituals · law and crime · state, politics and civic engagement · globalisation, climate and nature                                     | Partizipialattribute, Gerundiv and nominalised adjectives · Passiv and Passiversatzformen · subjektive Modalverben · Modalpartikeln · prefix verbs · the full list of genitive prepositions · idioms and register                                                                          |

Boundary cases: C2.1 uses wegen / aufgrund / trotz / infolge only as the noun-phrase partners of conjunctions in the nominal-style
unit (the reformulation task of Goethe C2 Schreiben 1); the systematic treatment of genitive prepositions is C2.2's. Migration
and identity were proposed for C2.1 but only weakly supported, so C2.1 does not teach them.

## Overlap with B2.1, B2.2 and C1.1

B2.1, B2.2 and C1.1 were researched in parallel from other course books and were merged after the C2.1 unit list was fixed. Their
syllabi touch some of the same grammar as five C2.1 units. The vocabulary does not overlap (see above); the grammar does:

| C2.1 unit                 | Also taught in                                                | How C2.1 differs                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| u2 Noun-verb combinations | C1.1 u4, B2.1 / B2.2 (`noun-verb-phrases`)                    | C2.1 uses its own tag and a different set of combinations (Stellung beziehen, unter Beweis stellen, zum Ausdruck bringen …)  |
| u3 Contrast and cause     | C1.1 u1 (hingegen), B1.1 / B1.2 (während, indem)              | adds wohingegen, indes, zumal, nämlich, dadurch dass                                                                         |
| u4 Consecutive, Futur II  | C1.1 u1 (folglich, somit), C1.1 u7 (so … dass, zu … als dass) | derart / dermaßen … dass, infolgedessen, mithin; **Futur II is new**                                                         |
| u5 Konjunktiv I           | C1.1 u3 and B2.1 / B2.2 (`konjunktiv-1`)                      | C2.1 treats it with research reporting: shifted time words, reported questions, hedging verbs; own tag `c21-reported-speech` |
| u6 Nominal style          | C1.1 u10 (`nominal-style`)                                    | extends it with im Falle, bei, durch and reformulation dialogues; own tag `c21-nominal-style`                                |
| u9 Concession             | C1.1 u5 (obgleich, wenngleich, so … auch)                     | adds allerdings, freilich, indessen, wie dem auch sei and the concession/rebuttal moves of a debate                          |

The overlap is real: a learner who followed C1.1 meets Konjunktiv I and nominal style again, now at C2 depth and with new vocabulary.
If the grammar overlap should go, units 5, 6 and 9 are the candidates to be replaced by topics from the C2.2 list or from later
Goethe C2 material; the vocabulary and games of the other units are not affected.

Not covered in C2.1 on purpose, because the sibling thread teaches them: Partizipialattribute, Passiv and its substitutes,
subjektive Modalverben, Modalpartikeln, Präfixverben (trennbar / untrennbar), the full set of genitive prepositions, idioms.

## Engines

C2.1 uses flashcards, quiz, memory, listen, spell, sentence, gap fill, sort it, conjugation (generated tables in
`c2-1/helpers.ts`: Konjunktiv I `k1Table` / `k1PerfectTable`, Futur II `futur2Table`, hindsight with a modal verb
`k2ModalPastTable`), blitz and dialogue. The clock and market engines are not used, and no pronunciation game was written:
the Goethe C2 description asks for varied intonation, but the speech synthesis in the browser cannot be trusted to model it.

## Grammar hints

Ten mini lessons were written for the new grammar tags (`c21-word-formation`, `c21-noun-verb`, `c21-contrast-cause`,
`c21-consecutive`, `c21-futur-2`, `c21-reported-speech`, `c21-nominal-style`, `c21-unreal`, `c21-text-cohesion`,
`c21-concession`). The Konjunktiv I and nominal-style gap fills carry a `why` sentence for each wrong option.
